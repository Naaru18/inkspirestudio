Before You Book

How much does a custom tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, complexity, placement, and style. Minimum charge is ₹1,500. Custom designs start from ₹3,000. Full sleeves from ₹25,000. We provide a detailed quote during your free consultation.

How do I prepare for my first tattoo?

Eat a good meal 2 hours before, stay hydrated, avoid alcohol for 24 hours, moisturize the skin area, wear comfortable clothing that provides easy access. Get good sleep the night before.

Do you charge for consultations?

All consultations are completely free of charge. You can visit the studio in person, video call, or WhatsApp us your reference images and we'll provide honest design feedback and pricing.

What is your cancellation/rescheduling policy?

We require 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. A 20% booking deposit is required for sessions over ₹5,000, which is fully refundable with sufficient notice or transferable to another date.

Can I get tattooed if I have sensitive skin?

Yes, in most cases. We recommend a patch test for highly sensitive or allergy-prone skin. Our inks are vegan-friendly and formulated for sensitive skin types. Discuss your concerns during consultation.

How long does healing take?

Surface healing takes 2–3 weeks. Complete deep healing takes 3–6 months. We provide a detailed aftercare kit and guide with every tattoo. Free touch-up is offered within 3 months of your session.
