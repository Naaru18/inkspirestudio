Hygiene & Safety Standards

Sterilization Process

All tools are sterilized using autoclave systems and medical-grade disinfectants. We follow strict single-use policies for needles.

Premium Equipment

We use high-end, industry-certified tattoo machines, ink, and disposable supplies to ensure precision and zero contamination risk.

Safety Protocols

Artists wear gloves, masks, and follow WHO-standard hygiene guidelines to maintain a completely safe environment.

Certified Artists

Our artists are professionally trained and certified, ensuring safe techniques, proper handling, and expert-level artistry.

Aftercare Guidance

We provide detailed aftercare instructions and support to ensure proper healing and long-lasting tattoo quality.

Clean Environment

Our studio is regularly sanitized with hospital-grade cleaning procedures to maintain a hygienic and safe workspace.
