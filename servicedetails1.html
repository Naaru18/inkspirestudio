Frequently Asked Questions

Does getting a tattoo or piercing hurt? ▼
Pain depends on placement and your tolerance. Most clients describe it as mild to moderate discomfort, and our artists ensure the process is as comfortable as possible.
How long does a tattoo last? ▼
Tattoos are permanent, but proper aftercare and occasional touch-ups help maintain their quality and sharpness over time.
Is it safe to get a tattoo or piercing? ▼
Yes. We follow strict hygiene protocols including sterilized tools, disposable needles, and certified equipment to ensure complete safety.
Can I remove or modify my tattoo later? ▼
Yes. Tattoos can be lightened or removed with laser treatments, and we also offer cover-up tattoo solutions for redesigning existing tattoos.
How long does the healing process take? ▼
Healing typically takes 2–4 weeks depending on the size and placement. Proper aftercare is essential for faster healing.
